Frequently Asked Questions.

How does the Honor System work?
Brosky relies on community verification. Every member agrees to the code of conduct. If a member is reported and verified to be in violation, they are permanently removed from the network.
Is my location data private?
Yes. We only show your approximate proximity (e.g., "within 2km") to other verified brothers. Your exact coordinates are never shared or stored longer than necessary for the Radar to function.
Who is Brosky for?
Brosky is built for ambitious men who value time, privacy, and high-utility connections. It's for the builders, the earners, and the brothers who want to coordinate in the real world.
