Frequently Asked Questions.

How does the Honor System work?
Brosky relies on community verification. Every member agrees to the code of conduct. If a member is reported and verified to be in violation, they are permanently removed from the network.
Is my location data private?
Yes. We only show your approximate proximity (e.g., "within 2km") to other verified brothers. Your exact coordinates are never shared or stored longer than necessary for the Radar to function.
Who is Brosky for?
Brosky is built for ambitious men who value time, privacy, and high-utility connections. It's for the builders, the earners, and the brothers who want to coordinate in the real world.
Will I get notifications when the app is closed?
Yes. Brosky uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver native push notifications to your Android or iOS device even when the app is completely closed. You'll never miss a message from your Brohood.
What happens if I block someone?
Blocking is immediate and bidirectional. Once you block a Bro, they instantly disappear from your Feed, Radar discovery list, and Messages inbox. You will no longer receive any communications from them.
Is there a free plan?
Yes. The core Brosky experience is free. Feed, Radar, Huddles, and Direct Messaging are all included. A Bro Premium tier with expanded features and no limits is coming soon.
